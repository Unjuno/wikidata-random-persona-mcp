# Random Persona original brand art

Created with the image-generation tool on 2026-10-28 for this project. All figures are original symbolic designs, not documentary portraits or replicas of existing characters. They are not illustrations of the live draw's subjects. The LP states this beside the hero and in its footer.

Art direction: a celestial library across time, a luminous cyan portal, fine gold astronomical mechanisms, midnight navy and violet, painterly anime-inspired cinematic realism. No logos, text, recognizable IP, specific historical likeness, or copied costumes. Japanese typography is actual HTML/CSS, never baked into images.

The category deliverables are 600×800 and 320×427. WebP quality 79, method 6, Lanczos resampling/crop, no metadata. Combined image bytes are about 1.8 MB; largest file is 273 KB. `picture`/`srcset` select appropriate hero compositions and category sizes. Hero uses high fetch priority, categories are lazy-loaded; headings and CTA render without waiting for images. Images have descriptive alternative text. Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Final web assets are committed in `public/assets/`; the build embeds the exact bytes in the Sites Worker so no external image hosting or missing build asset is required. Source PNGs were generated by the image tool and retained in the session outputs; optimized committed WebPs are the deployment originals. The named archetypes and their short demo lines are newly written fiction, with no QIDs or fabricated Wikimedia claims.
